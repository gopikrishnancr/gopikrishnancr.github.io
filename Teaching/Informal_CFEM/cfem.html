Foundations of computational finite element methods

Course overview

TThis course provides an introduction to the computational aspects of finite element methods. Using prototype problems such as the Poisson and heat equations, the Matlab-based implementations of several finite element methods are explained. A very brief theoretical side is also covered as and when required in the class, without any detailed proofs.
