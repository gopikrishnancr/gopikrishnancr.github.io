Orindary differential equations (MA5009)

Course overview

A detailed exposition of ordinary differential equations is provided. The standard tools and techniques for solving ODEs as well theorical results are also presented. The course covers general theory of first and second order equations, systems of linear equations, series solutions, and dynamical systems.
