Research outline


I focus on numerical analysis and mathematical modelling. A brief list of topics on interest are presented below.
  • Mathematical modeling using multi-phase mixture theory and non-linear elasticity theory of complex biological problems.
  • Scientific computing using appropriate combinations of finite element and finite volume methods.
  • Design and numerical analysis of finite element and finite volume schemes.
  • Convergence analysis, existence and uniqueness type reulsts.
I am open to collaborations on problems with a strong motivation from natural sceinces. The problems should also have a scope for scientiic computing and numerical analysis.
