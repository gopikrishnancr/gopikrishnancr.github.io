Hi, I am Dr Gopikrishnan Chirappurathu Remesan, an assistant professor in mathematics, IIT Palakkad.

My research interests include numerical analysis, mathematical biology, and scientific computing. Specifically, I focus on analysis of finite element based numerical implementation of model problems from biological sciences. I am passionate about nurturing young talents and inspiring students to pursue pure sciences.
