Lax-Richtmyer Theorem and Stability

Let \(X, Y, X_h, Y_h\) be normed linear spaces. Suppose \(A : X \rightarrow Y \) and \(A_h :X_h \rightarrow Y_h\) be invertible bounded linear operators. The continuous problem seeks \(u \in X\) such that \(Au = y\) and the discrete problem seeks \(y_h \in Y_h\) such that \(A_hx_h = y_h\), \(y_h = R_{Y_h}y\), \(R_{Y_h} : Y \rightarrow Y_h\) is an interpolation operator.
