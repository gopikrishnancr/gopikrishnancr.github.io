                <h5> Research </h5>
                <h4 style="line-height:0px;display:inline"> Interests: </h4>
				<ul class="int">
				<li> Mathematical modeling using multi-phase mixture theory 
				and non-linear elasticity theory of complex biological problems.
				</li> 
				<li> Scientific computing using appropriate combinations of 
				finite element and finite volume methods.
				</li>
				<li> Design and numerical
                analysis of finite element and finite volume schemes.
				</li>
				<li>Convergence analysis of models that lead to a proof of
                existence (possibly uniqueness) of solutions.
				</li>
				</ul>
		    
		    I am open to collaborations on problems with a strong motivation from natural sceinces. The problems should also have a scope for scientiic computing and numerical analysis.
